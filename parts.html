Parts Questions

Asked all the time.

What lithium batteries do you install?

Bolt Energy USA lithium packs for 36V, 48V, and 72V carts — 24 to 70+ mile ranges, 12-year transferable warranty, compatible with most major brands including Club Car, EZ-GO, Yamaha, and Evolution. We also fit Eco Battery packs when that suits your cart better or a Bolt size is on backorder.

How much is a lithium conversion?

Most conversions run $2,200–$3,500 installed depending on the pack and your cart. Try the service estimator for a ballpark, or request a quote with your cart's details for an exact number.

Can you install parts on a cart I bought elsewhere?

Absolutely — batteries, wheels, lift kits, and accessories on most makes and models, regardless of where the cart came from.

How long does an install take?

Most battery conversions and accessory installs are same-day or next-day once parts are in hand. Special-order GTW parts typically arrive within a week.
