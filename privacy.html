Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Aledo Golf Carts ("we," "us," "our") operates aledogolfcarts.com. This policy explains, in plain English, what information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

Information we collect

The personal information we collect is what you choose to send us through our website forms or by phone, text, or email: your name, email address, phone number, and details about what you're interested in (a cart, a part, service, or financing). We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Our web host may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for security and reliability. This site does not use advertising trackers.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry — confirming availability, scheduling a test drive or service visit, or quoting a part.
  • To communicate with you about your purchase, trade-in, financing application, or service work.
  • Nothing else. We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Calls and text messages (TCPA consent)

When you submit a form with your phone number and check the consent box, you agree that Aledo Golf Carts may contact you by phone call and text message (SMS) at the number you provided, including through automated technology, regarding your inquiry. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. You may also revoke consent by calling us at (817) 776-2175.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to us by our website hosting provider's form service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and our contact page embeds a Google Map. Each of these providers processes only the minimum data needed to do its job, under its own privacy policy. If you apply for financing, the information you provide for that application is shared with the lender solely to process your application.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence and customer records as long as they're useful for serving you and as required for normal business record-keeping. To ask what we have on file or to have it removed, call (817) 776-2175 or use the contact form.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
