Financing Questions

Asked all the time.

How does financing work?

Pick a cart, run the payment calculator to find a comfortable monthly number, then apply online with Sheffield Financial or Dealer Direct. We work with both, so a no from one is not the end of it. Approved-credit terms run 24–60 months, and trade-ins can serve as your down payment.

Will checking my rate hurt my credit?

Prequalifying with Sheffield is a soft credit check and does not affect your score. Only a full application — once you have decided to go ahead — is a hard check, the same as any other loan.

Can my old cart be the down payment?

Yes. Run it through the trade-in estimator for an instant ballpark, and we'll confirm the exact figure when you bring it by.

What if I've been declined before?

Try the other lender. The two run different programmes and look at applications differently, which is the whole reason we work with both. It costs you nothing to find out.

Can I finance a repair rather than a cart?

Often, yes — larger jobs like a lithium conversion can go through the same lenders. Tell us what the cart needs on the service page and we'll let you know.
