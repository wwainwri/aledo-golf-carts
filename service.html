Service Questions

Asked all the time.

What do you service?

Batteries and lithium conversions, tires and wheels, brakes, tune-ups, accessory installs, and diagnostics — on most makes and models, including Club Car, EZ-GO, Yamaha, and Evolution.

How much will my repair cost?

The service estimator above adds up typical ranges for your exact list instantly. Final pricing is confirmed after we've looked at the cart — no surprise line items.

Do you service carts you didn't sell?

Absolutely — bring it by regardless of where it came from. Most jobs are same-day or next-day once parts are in hand.

How long will it take?

Most jobs are same-day or next-day once the parts are in hand. We diagnose the day the cart arrives and call you with a firm price before any work starts.

Can I spread the cost of a big repair?

Often, yes — the same lenders we use for carts can cover larger jobs like a lithium conversion. See financing for how that works.
