Common Questions

Straight answers, no runaround.

How much does a golf cart cost?

New four-passenger carts typically start around $10,995 and six-passenger models around $14,500; used carts vary with age and condition. Try the payment calculator to see monthly numbers, or check the current inventory for live pricing.

Do you take trade-ins?

Yes — most carts qualify. The trade-in estimator gives you an instant ballpark, and we confirm an exact number when you bring the cart by.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, flexible financing on approved credit. Run any price through the payment calculator to see what a monthly payment looks like before you ever talk to a lender.

Is a lithium battery upgrade worth it?

For most owners, absolutely. Lithium charges faster, runs longer, weighs less, and needs essentially zero maintenance. We install Bolt Energy USA lithium batteries backed by a 12-year transferable warranty — typical upgrades run $2,200–$3,500 installed.

Do you service carts you didn't sell?

Absolutely — batteries, tires, brakes, electrical, and routine maintenance on most makes and models. The service estimator gives you a ballpark before you load up the trailer.
